## VSWIR and TIR in this community

**VSWIR — visible to shortwave infrared:** studying reflected sunlight across wavelengths can help characterize materials and vegetation. **TIR — thermal infrared:** studying emitted energy supports temperature-related analysis. Temperature retrieval also depends on factors such as atmosphere and surface emissivity.

NASA describes EAGLE as **Explorer for Artemis Geology, Lunar, and Earth**, with future VSWIR and TIR missions. Use the [NASA EAGLE overview](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/eagle/) for mission facts; this community does not set mission specifications or schedules. Existing EMIT and ECOSTRESS observations can support learning today. They must be labeled by their actual sensor, never presented as EAGLE observations.

## Three practical starting points

| Path | Read | Make a small contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand the missions | [NASA EAGLE overview](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/eagle/) | Improve a glossary entry with a source and example |
| Explore imaging spectroscopy | [NASA EMIT](https://earth.jpl.nasa.gov/emit/) | Propose an accessible resource and explain its prerequisites |
| Explore thermal observations | [ECOSTRESS](https://ecostress.jpl.nasa.gov/) | Review how a tutorial distinguishes radiance from temperature |

These are official external resources, not community-maintained projects. Some data services require an Earthdata account or additional tools; read each provider's access instructions before downloading anything.

## Bring a resource

Use the [education proposal form](https://github.com/CONNECT-EAGLE/community/issues/new?template=educational-resource.yml). Include the audience, prerequisite knowledge, learning outcome, link, and reuse terms. Prefer a focused resource that helps someone complete a real task. Clearly label synthetic examples and provide text alternatives for figures.

## Terms to build together

The open glossary tasks cover reflectance, radiance, spectral signature, imaging spectroscopy, brightness temperature, land-surface temperature, emissivity, and atmospheric correction. Keep definitions short, explain differences with examples, and cite authoritative sources. See [beginner tasks](OPPORTUNITIES.md#beginner).
