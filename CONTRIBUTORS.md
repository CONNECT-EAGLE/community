# Contributors and credit

Every contribution should have an identifiable result: a merged PR, a substantive review, a verified example, a tutorial, a dataset validation note, or another linked artifact. These are useful portfolio records without scores or leaderboards.

- **Community setup:** [Fares Alhezaimi (@falhezaimi)](https://github.com/falhezaimi), current organization setup contact.
- **Code and documentation:** see [GitHub contributors](https://github.com/CONNECT-SBG/testrepo/graphs/contributors) and merged pull requests.
- **Scientific and non-code work:** acknowledge contributors in the relevant PR, review note, or project README, with their agreement.
- **Historical work:** retain existing author attribution and commit history; see [History](HISTORY.md).

Project maintainers choose the project's citation and publication authorship criteria. Community membership, issue assignment, or an automated commit is not a scientific authorship claim. Do not list mentors, affiliations, endorsements, or contributed work that have not been confirmed.
