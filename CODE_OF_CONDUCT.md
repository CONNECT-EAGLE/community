# Code of conduct

Welcome people across disciplines, experience levels, nationalities, and identities. Critique ideas and evidence respectfully. Credit contributions, explain unfamiliar terminology, and help newcomers participate.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and deliberate misrepresentation of authorship are unacceptable. Maintainers may moderate content, warn participants, or restrict participation proportionately. A person involved in a complaint must not decide its outcome; a second uninvolved reviewer should handle appeals.

**Reporting status:** a dedicated private community reporting channel and second moderator still need owner designation. Do not post sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Use GitHub's report-abuse controls for platform abuse. This gap is recorded in the launch checklist and must be resolved before broad recruitment; no nonexistent private mailbox is advertised.
