# Historical CONNECT-SBG work

The organization began as CONNECT-SBG. Its original drought notebooks, backup/filtered variants, report, comparison artifact, and environment snapshot remain at this repository's root with their original history. They are historical research material; their current maintenance and scientific validation status are unconfirmed.

The baseline before the EAGLE foundation is [commit c11d821](https://github.com/CONNECT-EAGLE/testrepo/tree/c11d82179bb19bac5c0399dfcaaaab3a4d140600). Existing scientific files have not been deleted, renamed, rewritten, or relicensed. New foundation licenses do not apply retroactively to them.

The separate [inthisclimate/CONNECT-SBG repository](https://github.com/inthisclimate/CONNECT-SBG) contains related historical research. It remains under its own owner's control and is not presented as a registered EAGLE project.

The organization URL stays `CONNECT-SBG` for continuity. C.O.N.N.E.C.T. EAGLE is the current community identity. [Return to current work](README.md).
