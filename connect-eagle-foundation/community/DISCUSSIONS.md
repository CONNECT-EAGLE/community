# Discussion guidance

After assigning moderators, enable GitHub Discussions in the community repository with these categories:

- **Start here / Q&A:** installation, registration and beginner questions; accept useful answers.
- **Project showcase:** a short problem statement, project link, reproducible result and collaboration request.
- **Scientific ideas:** hypotheses, methods, uncertainties and requests for review.
- **Tools and tutorials:** practical resources with environment requirements and attribution.
- **Education and careers:** mentoring, learning groups, training and public opportunities.
- **Announcements:** maintainer-led releases and meeting notices.

Use issues for actionable bugs and scoped work; use discussions for open questions and proposals. Keep unpublished personal information out of public posts. Discussion activation and moderation staffing are launch tasks, not implied by adding this document.
