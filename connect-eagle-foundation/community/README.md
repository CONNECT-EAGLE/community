# C.O.N.N.E.C.T. EAGLE

**Collaborative Nexus for Networking, Education, and Career Training Supporting EAGLE VSWIR and TIR.**

A collaborative community for Earth-observation research, education, professional development, open tools and shared scientific inquiry around the EAGLE VSWIR and TIR ecosystem.

GitHub provides contribution and versioning infrastructure; the community is broader than GitHub. Members may showcase work, find collaborators, share useful tools, discuss research ideas, mentor students and explore career paths. Projects remain in their owners' repositories.

This is an independent community initiative. It is not presented as NASA's official EAGLE organization, and registration does not imply agency endorsement, funding or validation.

Start with an existing project: `connect-eagle init`, `connect-eagle doctor`, then `connect-eagle submit`. You can also contribute documentation, educational material or a working-group question. The initial release prioritizes this contribution loop; a website and other community services come later.
