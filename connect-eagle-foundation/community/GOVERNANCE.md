# Governance

## Initial operating model

The project founder coordinates launch. Each repository has named maintainers responsible for review, releases and support. Registry maintainers verify representational authority and scientific relevance; registration is not a peer-review certification.

Before public launch, appoint at least two community maintainers, an uninvolved escalation contact and a registry review owner. Publish names and reliable private contact routes. These roles remain unassigned in this engineering foundation.

Use ordinary issues or Discussions for proposals. A material schema or governance change needs a written proposal, one week for community feedback when practical, and approval from two uninvolved maintainers. Routine fixes follow repository review. Record decisions and rationale in the relevant PR or discussion.

Contributors retain copyright and repository ownership. Community participation does not create employment, institutional affiliation or an obligation to transfer work. Scientific authorship follows actual contributions and the norms of the project; registry participation alone does not earn authorship.

Resolve disagreements first with the relevant maintainer, then with an uninvolved community maintainer. Code-of-conduct matters use the private reporting process.
