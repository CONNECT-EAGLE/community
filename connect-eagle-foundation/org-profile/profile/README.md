# C.O.N.N.E.C.T. EAGLE

**Collaborative Nexus for Networking, Education, and Career Training Supporting EAGLE VSWIR and TIR**

An independent community connecting Earth-observation research, open tools, education, networking, and career development. Students, scientists, developers, and educators can contribute; projects keep their existing owners.

**[START HERE](https://github.com/CONNECT-SBG/testrepo/blob/main/START_HERE.md) · [Projects](https://github.com/CONNECT-SBG/testrepo/blob/main/PROJECTS.md) · [Open opportunities](https://github.com/CONNECT-SBG/testrepo/blob/main/OPPORTUNITIES.md)**

**VSWIR** uses reflected visible-to-shortwave-infrared light to study surface properties. **TIR** uses emitted thermal-infrared energy for temperature-related studies. Read [NASA's EAGLE overview](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/eagle/) for official mission information. This community is not NASA's official EAGLE organization.

## Get involved

- **First contribution:** choose a [beginner task](https://github.com/CONNECT-SBG/testrepo/blob/main/OPPORTUNITIES.md#beginner).
- **Already have a project?** [Connect it](https://github.com/CONNECT-SBG/testrepo/blob/main/SUBMIT_PROJECT.md) without moving it.
- **Prefer non-code work?** Contribute documentation, scientific review, educational resources, figures, or dataset validation.
- **Need help?** [Ask a question](https://github.com/CONNECT-SBG/testrepo/blob/main/COMMUNITY.md).
