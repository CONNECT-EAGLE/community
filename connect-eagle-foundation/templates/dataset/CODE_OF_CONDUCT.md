# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect across scientific background, career stage, nationality and identity. Make collaboration welcoming to students and newcomers. Critique methods and evidence, credit prior work, and distinguish observations from interpretations.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, disclosure of another person's private information, and deliberate misrepresentation of authorship are unacceptable. Keep discussions relevant and resolve disagreements professionally.

To report a concern, contact a named repository maintainer privately through the contact they publish on their profile. Do not put sensitive reports in public issues. Before community launch, governance must designate at least two named moderation contacts and publish a private reporting channel; this foundation does not invent one.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or remove access for repeated or severe violations. Decisions should be proportionate, explained privately where appropriate, and reviewed by a second uninvolved maintainer. A maintainer involved in a complaint must recuse themselves. Participants may request review by another uninvolved maintainer.
